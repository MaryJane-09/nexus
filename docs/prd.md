## 4. Product principles

1. **Clarity over complexity** — show what matters without creating unnecessary process.
2. **Context belongs with work** — decisions and rationale should be connected to projects and tasks.
3. **Ownership should be explicit** — every important action should have a responsible person.
4. **Intelligence should assist, not obscure** — recommendations must be understandable and controllable.
5. **Useful defaults, flexible workflows** — teams should be productive without extensive configuration.
